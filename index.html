Theoretical condensed matter physics

Topology, frustration and skyrmions in magnetic matter.

I'm H. Diego Rosales, Independent Researcher at CONICET (IFLySiB) and Professor at the Faculty of Engineering, Universidad Nacional de La Plata, Argentina. My group studies how collective magnetic textures emerge, order and move, using analytical methods and large-scale simulations.

Research interests

What I work on

All research lines →

  • Magnetic skyrmions

    Topological spin textures in chiral and frustrated magnets: how they form crystals, fluids and glasses, and how they respond to fields, anisotropy and disorder.

  • Frustration & spin liquids

    Classical and quantum spin liquids on kagome, honeycomb and triangular lattices, order-by-disorder and state selection.

  • Strongly correlated systems

    Low-dimensional antiferromagnets and electronic systems: spin tubes, ladders, magnetoelastic chains and Hubbard models.

  • Numerical methods

    Monte Carlo, simulated annealing, spin dynamics and machine learning to build detailed phase diagrams.
