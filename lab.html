Interactive physics

Small simulations you can play with in the browser. They are simplified models, built to give intuition about the systems I study.

Simulation 01

Skyrmions on a racetrack

A current pushes the skyrmions along the track. Because they are topological objects, they do not move straight: the gyrotropic (Magnus-like) force deflects them sideways, the skyrmion Hall effect. Lower the damping α and the deflection grows. Click the track to add skyrmions or pinning sites.

Sign sets the direction of motion.
Smaller α → larger skyrmion Hall angle.
Random thermal kicks.

Model: each skyrmion is a particle obeying the Thiele equation G ẑ × v + αD v = F, with a driving force from the current, short-range repulsion between skyrmions and from the edges, attractive pinning sites and thermal noise. Arrows show the in-plane spin component, coloured by angle.
